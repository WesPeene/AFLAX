Why It Matters

The network doesn't stop at graduation.

Alumni go on to serve, build careers, and lead all over the country. Staying connected keeps that network useful — for new grads finding their footing, for cadets looking for a mentor, and for a program that runs on people who stay involved.

Get Involved

Four ways to stay in the fight.

01

Mentor a Cadet

Connect with current players navigating the same balance of Academy life, athletics, and what comes after graduation that you once did.

02

Join the Board

Friends of Air Force Lacrosse is run by alumni and parent volunteers. There's always room for more hands on deck — reach out if you want in.

03

Come to a Game

Nothing beats being back on the sideline. Check the schedule and make it out to a home game when you're near the Academy.

04

Give Back

Financial support from alumni funds equipment, travel, and the extras that make the program competitive at the D1 level.

Stay In Touch

Update your info.

Update your contact info, your class year, and what you're up to now — it's how we build class notes, reunions, and the mentor network.
