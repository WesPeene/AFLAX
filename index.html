Our Mission

Bridging the Academy and the community.

The Friends of Air Force Lacrosse is a community of alumni, parents, staff, and supporters dedicated to the well-being of the USAFA Men's Lacrosse program.

We provide the resources and support necessary to forge leaders of character and keep the Falcons competing at the highest level — on the field and long after graduation.
